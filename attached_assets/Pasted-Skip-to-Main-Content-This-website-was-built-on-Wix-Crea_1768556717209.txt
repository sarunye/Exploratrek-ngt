Tips for planning   your adventure tour

Book your flights and accommodations in advance. This will help you to get the best prices. Pack light. You will be doing a lot of walking, so you don't want to be weighed down by heavy luggage. Bring sunscreen, insect repellent, and a hat. The sun and insects can be very strong in the desert. Be prepared for anything. The weather can change quickly in the desert, so it's important to be prepared for anything. I hope this helps!

Organized Packing List:

Clothing: Shawl or light jacket for cooler evenings, Swimwear for water activities, Warm jacket or hoodie for added warmth.

Footwear: Comfortable closed outdoor shoes for hiking and exploring, Sandals for relaxation at the camps.

Toiletries: Towel, ​Soap, ​Toothbrush, Toothpaste, Tissues

Food and Drinks:

Drinks and beverages (beers available at local shops; feel free to bring your preferred hard drinks).

Snacks such as crisps, biscuits, sweets, or cookies for on-the-go munching.

Accessories: Day pack or small bag to carry personal items, Camera, Power bank, Chargers

Sun Protection: Sunhat, Sunscreen, Sunglasses

Essentials: Insect repellent to keep pesky bugs away, Identification card for park access, Tip for the crew and guides, By organizing your packing list into related categories, you can easily locate and access the items you need during your adventure.

 

Please note that the package includes meals on safari, transportation in a 4X4 Land Cruiser, and 4 nights of accommodation at budget camps and hotels.

You will have the opportunity to visit Ngurunit Rock sliding Pools, experience the El-molo culture, and enjoy a touPark entry fees for citizens and residents are included, along with a knowledgeable tour guide to enhance your experience.

However, please be aware that en route lunch at designated stops on the first and last day is not included. Non-resident park entry fees, boat rides to El-molo, and the rock slide community fee are also not covered. Additionally, any items of personal use, tips for guides, and medical and travel insurance expenses are not included in the package.

Ensure you plan accordingly and consider these inclusions and exclusions when preparing for your adventure.
